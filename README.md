# Rise: Built to Rise

**Rise is a Phoenix marketing campaign identity and an artistic showcase.** It shows onboarders and new team members what Phoenix can do creatively. It has its own look: green, black and white, a circular bird monogram, and "Rise" lettering in gold with silver and green accents.

> **Notes**
> - Rise is **not a separate venture** and **not the Phoenix brand**. It is a campaign style guide inside Phoenix Mobile Advertising's work.
> - **The name "Rise" is not a registered trademark of ours, and nothing here claims one.** Other companies, including Green Thumb Industries, hold RISE marks. A name check is needed before Rise is used publicly as a name.
> - Rise and Phoenix stay separate. Phoenix's paper, ink and ember style is documented in [phoenix-portfolio](https://github.com/williamboyd1221-bit/phoenix-portfolio) and is never mixed into Rise.

## Purpose

1. **Campaign.** A "Built to Rise" look for marketing pieces that need a different voice from the Phoenix editorial style.
2. **Showcase.** A creative portfolio piece that onboarders and new team members can study.

## At a glance

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| Palette | Green, black, white |
| Lettering | "Rise" in gold, with silver and green accents |
| Monogram | Circular, with a bird |
| Theme | "Built to Rise" |
| Not allowed | Jock, athletic or sports styling |
