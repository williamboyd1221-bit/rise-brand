# Rise brand system

**Status: working draft.** Hex values below are **working values pending William's approval**. They were chosen to fit the rules (green, black, white, gold lettering with silver and green accents) and are not final.

**Scope.** Rise is a Phoenix marketing campaign style guide and a creative showcase for onboarders and new team members. It is not a separate venture and not the Phoenix brand. The name "Rise" is not a registered trademark of ours. Other companies, including Green Thumb Industries, hold RISE marks, so nothing here implies a trademark claim.

## Palette (working values)

| Role | Name | Hex (working) | Use |
|------|------|---------------|-----|
| Primary | Rise green | `#1f6b3a` | Fields, shapes, accents |
| Deep | Rise black | `#0b0d0c` | Backgrounds, body text |
| Light | Rise white | `#f7f8f6` | Backgrounds, reversed text |
| Lettering | Rise gold | `#c9a227` | "Rise" lettering only |
| Accent | Silver | `#b8bec2` | Thin rules, highlights |
| Accent | Green tint | `#3f9a5c` | Highlights, hover states |

Gold is for the Rise lettering only. It is not a general fill color.

## Type (working)

- **Lettering:** "Rise" as a custom or display wordmark in gold. Final lettering is pending approval.
- **Headlines:** a clean, strong sans-serif or a sturdy serif, set in white on black or black on white.
- **Body:** a plain readable sans-serif at comfortable sizes.
- Avoid script fonts and anything that reads as team or sports lettering.

## Monogram

- Circular, with a bird. It is close in spirit to the Phoenix bird but is the Rise mark, used in Rise materials only.
- Clear space: at least half the monogram's width on all sides.
- Minimum size: keep the bird legible. If it fills in, use a larger size.
- Versions: green on white, white on black, green on black.
- The final monogram artwork is not in this repo yet.

## Do

- Use green, black and white as the base of every piece.
- Set "Rise" lettering in gold with silver and green accents.
- Use the circular bird monogram.
- Keep "Built to Rise" as the theme line.
- Keep Rise pieces clearly their own thing.
- Include the Phoenix call to action when a piece is marketing: **Call 541-632-9043 or book a Business X-Ray.** No prices.

## Don't

- Don't use jock, athletic or sports styling: no jerseys, team type, stadium or locker-room looks.
- Don't mix Rise into Phoenix's paper, ink and ember ads, and don't apply Phoenix's rules to Rise.
- Don't use gold as a background or general color.
- Don't invent clients, testimonials, results or numbers.
- Don't present Rise as a registered trademark or as a separate company.

## Applications (examples)

- Campaign graphic: black field, white headline, gold Rise lettering, one green accent.
- Onboarding showcase slide: white field, green monogram, short "what we made and why" caption.
- Short video title card: black, gold lettering, silver rule.
